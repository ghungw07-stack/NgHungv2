# World Boss image sources

All source images below are published under CC0/public-domain terms on OpenGameArt.
The bot only scales and animates these source images inside its battle GIF renderer.

- `avaras-dragon.png`, `demon-bird.png`, `eldritch-idol.png` — “12 Public Domain Boss Sprites!”, titleknown: https://opengameart.org/content/12-public-domain-boss-sprites
- `orange-dragon.png` — “Dragon 1 (M484)”, Master484: https://opengameart.org/content/dragon-1-m484
